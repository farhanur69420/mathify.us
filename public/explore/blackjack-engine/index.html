How this engine works tap to open

Keyboard: A 2–9 enter a card, 0 / T / J / Q / K a ten, Backspace undo, D dealer, Y your hand, O other players, N new round, S new shoe.

Card counting only produces an edge against a physical shoe that is dealt down before it is shuffled — that means live-dealer tables. RNG blackjack (Stake Originals, most "instant" blackjack on Bet365 and elsewhere) reshuffles a full shoe before every hand, so the count is permanently zero and the only thing that can help you is exact strategy for the rules in force. This page tells you which situation you are in and still plays perfectly in both. It is an analysis tool for maths and probability, not advice to gamble. Gamble responsibly — and never with money you need.
